App icon color playground

One seed color in, two previews out: the everyday full-color icon, and the Android 13+ "themed icon" silhouette that the OS re-tints to match your wallpaper. Both are derived from the real Material 3 algorithm, using the Tonal Spot variant — the one that keeps colors visibly related to what you typed (Cairn's own app theme separately uses the more hue-shifting "Expressive" variant, which isn't a good fit for picking an icon color you can actually reason about). Three elements: disk, ring, center label — canvas is always the darkest tone, the disk right after it.
